One-time audio setup (Mac)
  1. Install BlackHole 2ch.
  2. Open Audio MIDI Setup and create a Multi-Output Device with your speakers + BlackHole.
  3. Set that Multi-Output as your Mac speakers, so you still hear the show.
  4. Come back here and press Start.
Subtitles will appear here.
Tip: keep the floating caption window on top of your drama. Drag it anywhere.
